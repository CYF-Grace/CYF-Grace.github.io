Bracelets & handmade

Blue Heart Friendship Bracelet

A handwoven friendship bracelet inspired by ocean waves, using layered blue and white tones to create a flowing pattern. This piece explores color rhythm, symmetry, and precision in small-scale textile work.

Purple Diamond Friendship Bracelet

A geometric friendship bracelet built around repeating diamond motifs in purple, black, and white. Designed to balance contrast and repetition while practicing consistency in knot spacing and pattern alignment.

Holiday Stripe Friendship Bracelet

A festive bracelet using red, green, white, and silver tones to create a compact striped pattern. This piece explores how seasonal color palettes can be translated into simple, wearable handmade designs.

Mint & Black Pattern Bracelet

A bold woven bracelet featuring alternating mint green and black motifs in a repeating symmetrical layout. Made to experiment with stronger contrast and sharper pattern definition in handmade knotwork.

Blue-Purple Woven Bracelet

A wider woven bracelet featuring blue, purple, and white in a repeating diagonal composition. The larger pattern scale allowed for more detailed color interplay and a more substantial finished texture.

Pastel Chevron Friendship Bracelet

A pastel chevron bracelet combining soft pinks and blues with darker accents for contrast. This design focuses on clean directional patterning and color transitions across a narrow woven band.

Rabbit Year Bracelet

A handmade bracelet designed to celebrate the Year of the Rabbit, featuring a rabbit-inspired centerpiece created through traditional Chinese knotting techniques. This piece combines rabbit-ear coils, blossom-inspired coils, seamless wrapped coils, an eight-strand round braid, blossom knots, flat-knot coils, and decorative cord wrapping to create a festive design that feels playful, delicate, and symbolic.

Dragon Year Bracelet

A handmade bracelet designed to celebrate the Year of the Dragon, featuring a dragon-inspired centerpiece built with traditional Chinese knotting techniques. This piece combines hollow dragon-scale knots, an eight-strand round braid, horn-shaped coils, dragon-scale coils, seamless wrapped coils, flat-knot coils, and decorative cord wrapping to create a festive red-and-gold design with strong symbolic character.

Snake Year Bracelet

A handmade bracelet designed to celebrate the Year of the Snake, featuring a compact decorative centerpiece in a festive red-and-gold palette. This piece combines dimple knots, hollow dragon-scale (snake-scale) knots, blossom-inspired coils, seamless wrapped coils, flat-knot coils, and decorative cord wrapping to create a design that feels refined, symbolic, and richly textured.

Lavender Knot Bracelet

A handmade bracelet featuring a soft lavender color palette with gold accents, centered around a decorative focal element. This piece combines ice-heart knots, blossom knots, an eight-strand round braid, flat-knot coils, seamless wrapped coils, and decorative cord wrapping to create a design that feels delicate, balanced, and refined.

Green Floral Knot Bracelet

A handmade bracelet featuring a deep green base with soft white floral accents, built around a compact decorative centerpiece. This piece combines concentric knots, blossom-knot coils, an eight-strand round braid, flat-knot coils, seamless wrapped coils, and decorative cord wrapping to create a design that feels natural, balanced, and finely detailed.

Green Mandala Knot Bracelet

A handmade bracelet featuring a deep green base with soft white accents, centered around a larger sculptural knot composition. This piece combines mandala knots, blossom knots, blossom-knot coils, an eight-strand round braid, flat-knot coils, seamless wrapped coils, and decorative cord wrapping to create a design that feels organic, layered, and carefully balanced.

Chinese Knot Keychains

A trio of handmade keychains exploring traditional Chinese knotting through color, texture, and compact form. These pieces combine snake knots, corn knots, phoenix-tail knots, and decorative cord wrapping to transform simple cords into small functional objects with playful visual character.

Woven Knot Coaster

A handmade coaster formed through an interwoven continuous knot structure, combining layered blue and white tones inspired by ocean waves. The piece balances symmetry, pattern, and structure while transforming traditional knotting techniques into a functional everyday object.

Transforming Origami Star

A handmade origami piece designed as an expandable eight-pointed star that can be reshaped through folding and rotation. Combining geometric symmetry with transformable structure, the piece explores how simple paper modules can create a playful object that shifts between decorative form and interactive motion.

Origami Flower Ball

A handmade modular origami sculpture assembled from repeated folded units into a geometric flower-ball form. The interlocking structure combines symmetry, color, and spatial layering to transform simple paper modules into a lightweight decorative object with strong dimensional presence.
