About this model

This model was created as a 3D aircraft design study, with attention to overall silhouette, surface transitions, and the balance between recognizable form and simplified digital styling.

The project helped strengthen my understanding of aircraft geometry, clean modeling workflows, and visual presentation in 3D design.
